Questions

The ones that actually come up.

Is this AI-generated?

The video ads are built with AI generation and directed frame by frame — that's how the turnaround is 72 hours instead of three weeks, and why the price isn't $5k. Statics are composed from your own product photography. If your brand can't use AI-generated people in its ads, say so at the brief stage and the pack is built entirely from your assets instead.

Do you run the ads too?

No. Creative only. You or your media buyer handle spend, targeting and budget. That's deliberate — it keeps the price where it is and means there's no conflict about what to blame when something doesn't work.

What do you need from me?

Product photos or shipped samples, your store URL, and access to your creative report if you have one. Roughly fifteen minutes up front, then a thumbs up or down on the angle sheet each cycle.

Who owns the files?

You do, on delivery, in perpetuity. Source files included on monthly plans. The only thing asked in return is permission to show the work — and that's opt-in, not assumed.

What if the ads don't perform?

Nobody can promise a return on an ad account they don't control, and anyone who does is selling you something. What's promised is volume, speed and craft — enough new angles that you're testing your way to a winner instead of waiting for one.

How many brands do you take?

Capacity is limited and one client per category at a time, so a direct competitor can't buy the same angles a week later.
